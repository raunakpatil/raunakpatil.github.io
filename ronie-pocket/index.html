Ronie Pocket offline AI assistant for Android

A private AI assistant and on-device image generator. No account, no cloud, no tracking. Chat, voice, memory and pictures stay on the phone, including in flight mode.

Download for Android Source on GitHub

Free, under Apache-2.0. This is the Android app. Ronie on this website is a separate 3D assistant in the browser.

On-device chat and image generation

Ask him to draw a cat astronaut in watercolour. About 45 seconds later the picture is in the chat, made by the phone's GPU. No server, no account, no credits, and the prompt is not logged anywhere. It works in flight mode.

Screenshots from a Pixel 9 Pro.

Drawing is unfiltered. You ask in chat ("draw", "paint", "make a picture of"), watch it take shape, then save it, share it, draw it again, or edit it. There is no daily quota, no watermark and no subscription. You can switch or delete drawing models in Settings, Models. The one thing he will not draw is anything sexual involving minors.

Why Ronie Pocket

Most AI assistants send every word you type to a server, ask you to sign up, and refuse half of what you ask. Ronie Pocket does none of that.

What it can do

A model on the phone

Gemma 4 runs on the phone's GPU. He is ready about 5 seconds after you open the app, and the first word of a reply comes in under a second.

Pictures on the phone

Ask in the chat and an image is generated on the GPU in about 45 seconds. The drawing model is SD-Turbo, an optional 1.8 GB download.

Unfiltered, with two limits

No topic filters and no lectures. A slider runs from clean to sweary. He will not help harm real people, and he refuses anything sexual involving minors.

Photos

Share a photo, receipt or screenshot and ask about it. A receipt becomes an expense card. This needs the full model.

Voice

Push-to-talk input, and replies spoken aloud by an on-device text-to-speech model. Ask by voice and he answers by voice.

Memory, on the phone

Tell him your sister is vegetarian, and later dinner ideas can take that into account. Memory is encrypted on the phone. You can see and delete everything he knows.

Tasks, reminders, calendar

Tasks, reminders, timers with a live countdown, alarms and your calendar. Every action shows a card with Undo, and anything risky asks first.

Web search, when you want it

No API key and no account. He searches, reads the pages and answers with numbered sources. Your name, emails, phone numbers and addresses are removed from the query before it leaves the phone.

Connectors

Add any remote Model Context Protocol server: notes, to-do apps, home automation. Each one is labelled with exactly where its data goes.

From any app

Share text or a link into Ronie to summarise it, explain it, turn it into a task, or remember it.

X-ray mode

In Labs, a live readout of what is happening inside the model while it writes.

The right model for the phone

Three curated models, chosen on first launch. 12 GB or more gets Gemma 4 E2B on the GPU. 8 GB gets Qwen3.5-2B. 4 GB gets Qwen3.5-0.8B.

Privacy

There is no account, no analytics SDK, no telemetry and no server of ours to send anything to. One Offline mode switch blocks every outgoing request. Each request is listed in the in-app activity log.

FAQ

Does it really work without internet?

Yes. After the one-time model download, chat, voice, memory, tasks, reminders, timers and alarms all work in flight mode. Only web search and connectors need a connection, and Offline mode switches those off.

Do I need an account or an API key?

No. There is nothing to sign up for. Web search uses DuckDuckGo without a key.

Is it actually uncensored?

Ronie has no topic filters or lectures, and you control his tone with a slider. The only things he will not do are help harm real people, for example weapon or poison instructions, and anything sexual involving minors.

Can it really generate images offline, and is that uncensored too?

Yes. Drawing uses SD-Turbo through stable-diffusion.cpp on the phone's GPU: about 45 seconds for a 512×512 picture on a Pixel 9 Pro, with nothing sent anywhere. It draws adult and nude content without filters. The only hard block is sexual content involving minors. The drawing model is an optional 1.8 GB download and its licence is for non-commercial use.

Which AI model does it use?

Phones with 12 GB of RAM or more get Gemma 4 E2B on the GPU. 8 GB phones get Qwen3.5-2B, and 4 GB phones get Qwen3.5-0.8B. Ronie recommends the right one on first launch.

How fast is it?

On a Pixel 9 Pro: ready in about 5 seconds, the first word in under a second, about 10 tokens per second. That is a comfortable reading speed. Older phones use a lighter model to stay quick.

How is this different from other local-LLM apps?

Most local-LLM apps are model runners: you pick a GGUF file and get a chat box. Ronie Pocket is an assistant. He has a personality and a voice, remembers you, uses tools (reminders, timers, calendar, web search, MCP), and works out of the box with a curated model. There is nothing to configure.

Is my data used to train anything?

No. Nothing is collected, so there is nothing to train on.
